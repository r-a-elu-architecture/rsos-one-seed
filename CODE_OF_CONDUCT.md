# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors who share its purpose (see `RESPONSIBLE_USE.md`).

- Be respectful and constructive; criticise ideas and results, not people.
- Report results honestly, including failures; never fabricate or selectively report data.
- Protect privacy: never submit personal, health or identifying data of real people.
- Respect consent in research involving people.

Harassment, discrimination and bad-faith conduct are not accepted. The maintainer may remove contributions or
contributors who violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainer (see `SECURITY.md`).

Inspired by the Contributor Covenant (contributor-covenant.org).
