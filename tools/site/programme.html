One set of operators, tested domain by domain.

The operators are defined independently of any domain. The research programme tests this in 24 domains, each with a first measurable test and its safeguards. The method is the same everywhere.

Purpose

RSOS grew from one seed: healing, transforming, signalling and evolving. It integrates the whole of human experience, including its shadow, so that what is integrated can be transformed rather than denied. Because its seed is healing, RSOS is used with consent and for the good of the person or system it works with, never to harm or coerce.

How the design carries it

  • Operations on people need their consent, checked by the compiler (E018).
  • Standing consent is valid only inside a declared network with members and laws (E021).
  • The Spine blocks predicted harm (E019).
  • The White Dragon admits only changes that are measurable, new and of good intention.
  • The author does not support using RSOS to deceive, manipulate, surveil or harm people, or to act on anyone without consent. Responsible use

Method in every domain

1. Bind the host

Its parts, channels, states and measures.

2. Pre-register

Predictions are fixed and committed before any data.

3. Strongest baseline

Run against the best available alternative, not a weak one.

4. Report every failure

Next to the successes, with code and data where permitted.

5. Replicate

An independent team repeats it before any strong claim.
