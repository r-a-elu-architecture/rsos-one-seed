# RSOS and SRC-X.5: every sealed claim, with its number, baseline and caveat

Machine-readable: [`claims.json`](claims.json). Every number comes from a sealed result file; each row links its source.

## In one paragraph

One shared decision selector (the seed) and the RSOS operator language, tested on real tools, real data and real models against each domain's standard method and against plain rollout; every test pre-registered and sealed before it ran; failures kept. Strongest results: CONF6 (AI-AI system: accuracy 1.000 vs 0.875 with 51 % fewer tokens vs plain rollout), CONF5 (AI agents: accuracy 0.95 vs 0.83 with 43 % fewer tokens vs plain rollout), CONF1 (beats the standard in four domains at Holm p < 1e-5), blind planning (318/320 exact; 48/48 on beacon-drawn tasks).

## What is new, and what is not claimed

The individual mechanisms (rollout, plan-then-execute, early-stopping self-consistency, scenario MPC, context compression) exist. What is new is one operator language bound unchanged across unrelated hosts (AI agents, AI-AI and AI-human communication, inventory, satellite downlink, energy, robotics), each tested sealed against that host's standard method and against plain rollout, with every claim, including failures, in an open, hash-verified record (SRC-X.5).

Not claimed: best in any single field; novelty of any single lever; results outside the sealed cells (production transfer not yet tested).
