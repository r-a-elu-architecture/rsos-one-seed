Edit a program as a painting

A real 12-module support agent, painted. Choose a guest, then click where it goes on its host: the head (what it takes in), the heart (how it works), the brain (what it knows) or near (beside it). Click the engine to turn it grey. Every number is measured by running the program.

Drag a guest onto its host: it snaps to the head, heart, brain or near. Or choose it and click a dashed circle. Drag a placed guest off its host to remove it.
