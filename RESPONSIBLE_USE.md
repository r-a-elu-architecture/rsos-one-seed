# Responsible use

RSOS grew from one seed: healing, transforming, signalling and evolving. It integrates the whole of human
experience, including its shadow, so that what is integrated can be transformed rather than denied. Because its seed
is healing, RSOS is used with consent and for the good of the person or system it works with, never to harm or
coerce.

The design carries this purpose. Consent and harm checks are compiled into the language (compiler errors
E018–E021); operations on people require their consent; the White Dragon admits only changes that are measurable,
new and of good intention.

The author does not support, and asks users not to use RSOS for:

- deceiving or manipulating people, or influencing them without their informed consent;
- surveillance or profiling of individuals;
- any use intended to harm people, including psychological harm;
- clinical, forensic or financial decisions about real people without validated methods, qualified
  professionals and the safeguards those fields require.

RSOS is research and development software. It is not a medical device and makes no clinical claims.

This statement expresses the author's intent and the project's norms. The software licence is Apache 2.0; see
`LICENSE`. Report misuse to the maintainer (see `SECURITY.md`).
