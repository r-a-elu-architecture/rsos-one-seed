## Diagnosis (after the results)

1. **A Father flaw.** Bear's "success" (a replication beating the incumbent) was easy to earn, so Father gave Bear
   about half of all evaluations, and the explorers got only 4–6 each. Next run: reward Bear only for replicating a
   candidate that is new, or give replication its own budget.
2. **T1 and T2 barely moved** within the primitives offered (+0.013 recall, +0.6 pp). Their limits look structural
   (information destroyed at 90 %; LAND's need rule), not a matter of parameters: they need new primitives, which is
   what the generator should grow next.
3. **The new operator's prior art:** disagreement between two readers as an error signal is known (committee and
   co-training methods). Here it was generated by the engine from the seed's primitives, not written by hand, and
   confirmed on a sealed hold-out.
