## The lesson Cosmology already measured

Its autonomous loop searched a **finite** registered catalog of lexical mechanisms and stopped: 0 of 3 champion
challenges accepted, alias catalog exhausted, information budget spent. A self-growing seed (level D) cannot draw
from a fixed list: it needs a **generator**. The seed's operator algebra (compose, nest, re-host) is that generator,
bounded by Law 0.
