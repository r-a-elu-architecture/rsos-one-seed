| 12 | 4,096 | 24–88 numbers (0.006–0.021×) | 10,920 (2.67×) |

In layer 2 every amplitude is readable (the full state is held and read back exactly). A structured quantum state of
20 qubits is held as a chain of 20 small nodes of at most 152 numbers instead of a million amplitudes, and expands
back into the whole with no loss. A random state has no such structure and does not compress (the chain is larger
than the state). Frame: this is the matrix-product-state (tensor-network) method; the result is that the owner's
"node that expands into the whole structure" is exact in layer 2 for structured states and fails for structureless
ones. The physical limits (Holevo, no-cloning) belong to layer 1 and are not tested here.
