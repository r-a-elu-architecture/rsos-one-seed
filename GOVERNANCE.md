# Governance

- **Maintainer and author:** R. A. Elu. The maintainer decides what is merged and released, and keeps the
  project's purpose (`RESPONSIBLE_USE.md`) and scientific standards.
- **Scientific standard:** claims are made only from pre-registered, reproducible results; failures are published
  with successes; strong claims wait for independent replication.
- **Records:** the whitepaper and its versions are deposited on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24304403); releases are
  tagged in this repository.
- **Changes to governance** are made by the maintainer and recorded in this file.
