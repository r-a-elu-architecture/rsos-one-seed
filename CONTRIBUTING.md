# Contributing

1. Open an issue describing the change before large work.
2. Experiments: write and commit a pre-registration (predictions, measures, pass rules) **before** any run; report
   every result with its hash (`experiments/common.save`), failures included.
3. Code: add tests; `python -m pytest` must pass.
4. Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`) to certify the Developer Certificate of Origin (developercertificate.org):
   you wrote the change or have the right to submit it under the project's licence.
5. Contributions are licensed under Apache 2.0, the project's licence.
6. Do not submit personal, health or identifying data of real people.
