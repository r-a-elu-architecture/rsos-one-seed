## Procedure

- For each task the owner writes two messages to an AI, in his own words, without copying the target text:
  **S** = his symbols / RSOS form, **P** = plain natural text. Order alternates: odd tasks S first, even tasks P first.
- He times each message with a stopwatch from first keystroke to done and reports seconds. Characters are counted.
- Each message is decoded by a fresh AI agent with no access to the targets, given the same short RSOS glossary in
  both arms, returning JSON: place, perspective, state, components, function, trigger, depth, order, gain.
- Accuracy = share of the target's fields recovered, scored field by field; targets, messages, decodes and scores
  are committed to `experiments/ht1/` so anyone can recheck the scoring.

## Prediction

- **K1 (pass rule):** on at least 4 of 6 tasks, S is faster (seconds) **and** S accuracy ≥ P accuracy.
- Descriptive: characters S vs P; total time; accuracy means.
