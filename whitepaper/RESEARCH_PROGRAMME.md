# RSOS Research Programme: one executable language across domains

**R. A. Elu** · Draft for the owner's review · 17 November 2026 · Related: whitepaper v1,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.24304403; code: github.com/r-a-elu-architecture/RSOS-Language

## Purpose

RSOS grew from one seed: healing, transforming, signalling and evolving. It integrates the whole of human
experience, including its shadow, so that what is integrated can be transformed rather than denied. Because its seed
is healing, RSOS is used with consent and for the good of the person or system it works with, never to harm or
coerce. Its design reflects this
purpose: consent and harm checks are compiled into the language (E018–E021), operations on people require their
consent, and the White Dragon admits only changes that are measurable, new and of good intention. The author does
not support the use of RSOS to deceive, manipulate, surveil or harm people, or to act on anyone without consent.

## 1. Thesis

RSOS operators are defined independently of any one domain; the host decides how a program behaves. The same small
set of operations (represent a component with its place, state and lineage; detect divergence; heal and improve
while healing; transplant a mechanism across domains at a set depth and order; govern by consent and law at compile
time) applies to AI agents, people, teams, organisations, markets and physical systems. The programme tests that
thesis domain by domain, with pre-registered predictions, real data and every failure reported.

## 3. Operator mapping (shared by all domains)

| RSOS operator | General function |
|---|---|
| Component / sphere | A unit with place, properties, state, interfaces and nested structure |
| Lineage | Where a component came from, what changed it, what it passes on |
| Green dot (signal) | Detects silent divergence between a shared reference and a copy |
| Heal / evolve-heal | Repairs only what diverged; keeps the better version when two differ |
| White Dragon transplant | Selects a part, a cross-domain mechanism, a depth and an order; predicts the gain |
| House, rooms, objects | Cognition built as executable mental models (perspective + composition of components) |
| Governance | Consent, standing consent in networks with laws, harm checks, enforced before execution |
| Keyboard layer | Composes programs at the speed they are seen |

## 5. Method in every domain

1. Bind the host: its parts, channels, states and measures.
2. Pre-register predictions before any data.
3. Run against the strongest available baseline, not a weak one.
4. Report every failure next to the successes; publish code and data where permitted.
5. Replicate with an independent team before any strong claim.

## 6. Ethics and governance

Person-facing work requires informed consent, compiled into the program itself (E018) and confirmed by the study's
ethics process. No clinical, forensic or financial claim is made before validated trials in that domain. Sensitive
data stays private by default. RSOS is research and development software, not a medical device.

## 7. Priorities

1. **Now:** A3 (head to head), keyboard learning phase, HT2.
2. **Next:** cross-model test; decision-making pilot (first human domain).
3. **Then:** one systems domain (finance reconciliation or socio-technical incidents).
4. **With partners:** computational psychiatry and neuroscience, under ethics approval.
